# Code of Conduct

We want participation in this organization's repositories to be respectful,
constructive, and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be courteous and assume good faith.
- Critique work and ideas, not people.
- Respect other people's time, privacy, and differing experience levels.
- Be honest about what is and is not verified.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Spam, or repeated disruptive behavior after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions, and may block users who
violate this code. Report concerns to **Timothy.Gregg@complete.tech**; reports
are handled privately.

This code is a short summary in the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
